01 Direct lines

There is no contact form on this page, deliberately. A form on a static site is a third party you have to trust with your project details, and a silent failure mode when it stops working. Email reaches a person.

Useful things to include: what the thing has to do, the deadline, and the constraint you are most worried about. If there is hardware involved, a model number and a photograph of the connector will save us both a week.
