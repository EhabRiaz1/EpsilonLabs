## Glossary & definitions

- **Act 1** = months 0–6 from today (2026-10-09 → 2027-04-09). The 90-day
  commitments live inside it.
- **Effort codes:** S = ≤2 founder-days, M = 3–10 founder-days, L = 11–30
  founder-days (spread over the stated window). CC-assisted building
  compresses the software share 10–20x; hardware/field time does not compress.
- **Warmed outreach domain** = 1 domain (e.g. epsilonlabs.io), 3–4 weeks of
  tool-managed warm-up before first real send. Outbound volume therefore
  starts ~week 5, not week 1 — the funnel math below accounts for this.
- **Payback order** = the volume-trigger sequencing in "Agent build order"
  below: an agent is built only when the measured volume that justifies it
  exists (replies/wk, quotes/mo).
- **Run rate** = trailing-30-day closed (invoiced) revenue, measured at the
  milestone date. "Day 90 run rate" = revenue invoiced in days 61–90.
- **Qualified pipeline** = deals where a scoping call has happened and a
  quote is out or scheduled.
- **Product-grade (E1)** = logged actions, one dashboard, restorable state,
  demoable to a prospect — NOT multi-tenant, NOT packaged, NOT documented
  for resale until a buyer exists.

## Targets (the number this plan exists to serve)

End state: $10–20k/month sustained. Honest staging — $10–20k/mo by day 90
from a cold start is not credible once domain warm-up (~4 wks) and the
founder-approval gate are priced in. Staged targets:

| Milestone | Day 90 | Month 5–6 |
|---|---|---|
| Closed run rate (trailing-30-day) | expected ≥ $3–4k/mo; stretch $6k/mo (requires closes back-loaded into month 3 or the funnel's upper half) | ≥ $12k/mo for 2 consecutive months → triggers Act 2 hiring (this is the single canonical hiring threshold) |
| Qualified pipeline | ≥ $15k | ≥ $30k |

**Day-90 miss protocol:** if day 90 lands below $3k closed AND below $10k
qualified pipeline, the funnel assumptions are declared falsified: replan
ceremony (re-run this review), E2 and Dream-25 pause until the replan.

Funnel model (falsifiable assumptions, reviewed monthly):
- Referrals: 10–15 past-client/network asks (Master Paints, YS companies,
  fuel-sector contacts) → 2–4 warm intros in 90 days.
- Dream-25: 25 accounts, ~8/mo touched deeply → 3–5 conversations by day 90.
- Outbound: 50/wk from week 5 ≈ 300–400 sends in window × 0.6–1.5%
  interested-reply (NOT raw-reply) → 2–4 conversations.
- Content/inbound: 0–2 conversations (compounds after the window).
- Total: ~8–14 conversations → ~6–10 quotes → 30–40% close → 2–4 closes,
  avg $2.5–6k → $6–15k closed in window, exiting at $3–6k/mo run rate
  depending on close timing.
- Mid-point check (~week 19): on-track assessment against the month 5–6
  Act 2 trigger, so the 3–4x ramp from day 90 is not unexamined.
- Weekly leading indicators (in the CEO brief): new conversations, quotes
  out, $ closed, interested-reply rate, referral asks made.

## Vision

### 10x Check
Epsilon Labs as its own first customer: the company runs on a named,
instrumented agent operations layer ("Epsilon OS") — pipeline, quoting,
triage, invoicing, daily CEO brief — which is then sold as the productized
AI-operations offer with the lab as living proof. The security ambition is
pulled forward behind a hard gate: Epsilon1 (sensor terminal) + the existing
digital-twin UI (live 3D command view) + a trusting industrial customer = a
mini-Lattice for industrial site security (ANPR + perimeter detection),
piloted at one existing customer's site. Anduril's playbook: accessible
buyers first, upmarket later.

### Platonic Ideal
A prospect hears of Epsilon from a past client, picks their problem on the
site, touches a live demo of the fix, writes one email — and receives a
pre-approved, no-claims acknowledgment within minutes, then a substantive
founder-approved reply the same business day. A call in days, a quote the
same day as the call. The founder's morning is one brief: leads scored
overnight, drafts awaiting approval, replies triaged, cash chased. Nothing
substantive leaves the building without founder judgment during the gated
period.

## The four tracks and the hours budget

Founder week ≈ 45–50h. Delivery (wedge projects only): ~30h. Growth: ~10h
(approvals/triage 3.5h, Dream-25 3h, war stories 2h, referrals 0.5h,
**E2 pilot pitch 1h** — the LOI work in months 1–3 lives here, folded into
referral/Dream-25 touches with the anchor-customer candidates).
Security-pilot track post-gate: capped 4h/wk taken from delivery — "priced
into quotes as reduced capacity" means quoted lead times lengthen by ~15%
during the pilot, not higher prices — **plus a one-time exception of 2–3
concentrated field-install days, flagged in the CEO brief the week they
happen.** The pilot delivery window is months 4–8. Build track (site rework,
E1, agents): CC-compressed, ~3h/wk of founder review, front-loaded in weeks
1–6. If any week busts the budget, the CEO brief flags it and Dream-25
pacing absorbs the slack (it degrades gracefully; approvals and delivery do
not).

Agent build order with triggers (payback-sequenced, not all-at-once):
1. Prospector + CRM-lite (week 2) — unblocks everything.
2. Inbound concierge on er@ (week 3) — auto-ack template pre-approved.
3. Reply triage — only when replies >10/wk.
4. Quote assembler — only when quotes >4/mo.

## ANPR / privacy stance (E2)

The pilot captures vehicle plates and perimeter events on the customer's
private premises. The customer is the data controller; Epsilon supplies the
system. Commitments: data minimization (plates + timestamps, no face
recognition), on-prem storage default, 30-day default retention, signage at
site entrances, written data-handling annex in the pilot agreement, and
compliance review against Pakistan's data-protection regime as in force at
contract time (budgeted: $200–500 legal, inside the E2 gated one-time
budget; annex drafting inside the post-gate 4h/wk). No public-space
surveillance, no resale of data.
