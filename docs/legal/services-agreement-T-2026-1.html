How to read this agreement

This is the standing set of terms behind every piece of work we do. The Proposal you accept says what we will build, when, and for how much; this agreement says how we work together while we do it. We have written it in plain English and kept it balanced, because we would rather you read it than skim it. Where “we” or “us” appears, it means Epsilon Labs; “you” means the Client.

1. Parties and Structure

1.1This Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made between Epsilon Labs AI Solutions (SMC-Private) Limited (CUIN 0290150), trading as Epsilon Labs (“Epsilon Labs”, “we”, “us”), and the organisation named as the client in a Proposal (the “Client”, “you”).

1.2Each Proposal you accept forms a separate contract made up of that Proposal and this Agreement, in the terms version stated in the Proposal. Accepting a Proposal is how this Agreement comes into force between us; no separate signature of this Agreement is needed.

1.3If documents conflict, they take priority in this order: (a) terms written specifically into the Proposal, including any special conditions; then (b) this Agreement. The terms of any purchase order, vendor registration form, supplier code or similar document you issue are expressly excluded and have no effect, even if we sign, acknowledge or invoice against it. A purchase order number may be quoted on our invoices for your administrative convenience only.

1.4We provide services to businesses only. You confirm that you are entering into each Proposal in the course of a business and not as a consumer.

2. Definitions

2.1In this Agreement the following words have these meanings:

(a)“Acceptance” means your acceptance of a Deliverable or Milestone under clause 8, including deemed acceptance; “Accepted” has a corresponding meaning.

(b)“Background IP” means all intellectual property owned or licensed by us before a Proposal or developed independently of it, including our libraries, firmware frameworks, board templates, tools, scripts, methods and know-how, and any general improvements to them.

(c)“Business Day” means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Lahore, Pakistan or in the place where the Client is established.

(d)“Change Request” means a request by either party to change the scope, Deliverables, timeline, acceptance criteria or fees of a Proposal.

(e)“Client Materials” means everything you or your contractors provide to us, including data, source code, documentation, designs, specifications, credentials, equipment, test hardware and site access.

(f)“Deliverables” means the software, firmware, hardware designs, documents, reports and other outputs we are to provide under a Proposal.

(g)“Hardware” means physical goods we supply under a Proposal, including printed circuit boards, assembled devices, enclosures, sensors, modules and purchased components.

(h)“Milestone” means a defined stage of work named in a Proposal, usually linked to a payment.

(i)“Portal” means our online client portal, through which Proposals are viewed and accepted, tickets and Change Requests are raised, and invoices, reports and files are shared.

(j)“Proposal” means a statement of work issued by us, identified by a proposal ID and version, describing the services, Deliverables, Milestones, acceptance criteria, timeline and fees.

2.2“Including” means including without limitation. “In writing” includes email and messages or approvals recorded in the Portal. Headings are for convenience only.

3. Proposals and Electronic Acceptance

3.1A Proposal is an offer open for the period stated in it (or 30 days if none is stated). Once issued, a Proposal version is frozen; any change produces a new version and withdraws the earlier one.

3.2You accept a Proposal in the Portal by typing the full name of the person accepting and entering the 6-digit code we email to the addressee of the Proposal. You agree that this process is a binding electronic signature on your behalf, with the same effect as a handwritten signature, and that the person accepting has authority to bind you.

3.3On acceptance we keep a record containing: the typed name; the email address the code was sent to; the date and time in UTC; the IP address and browser user agent; the proposal ID and version; the terms version; and a SHA-256 hash of the exact Proposal version together with the terms version. The record is shown in Schedule A. Both parties agree that this record is admissible evidence of acceptance and of the exact documents accepted. The IP address is kept only as evidence of the contract and is not used for analytics or marketing.

3.4If you prefer, a Proposal may instead be signed by hand or by a recognised e-signature service using the signature block in Schedule A. A Proposal may be signed in any number of counterparts, each of which is an original and which together form one document.

3.5We may publish new versions of this Agreement. A new version applies only to Proposals that state it; it never changes the terms of a Proposal you have already accepted.

4. Scope and Change Requests

4.1We will perform the services described in each Proposal with reasonable skill and care and in line with good industry practice. Anything not described in the Proposal is outside its scope.

4.2Either party may raise a Change Request. You raise one by opening a ticket in the Portal. We will respond with a written quote setting out the effect on fees, timeline and any other terms before any change work begins.

4.3A Change Request takes effect only once both parties confirm it in writing; approval of the quote in the Portal counts. We will not start work on a change until it is approved, and we will not charge for unapproved work.

4.4Small clarifications that do not affect fees, timeline or acceptance criteria may be agreed in a Portal ticket without a formal quote.

5.1You will, in good time and at your own cost:

(a)give us the access, credentials, information and documentation we reasonably need, and make sure it is accurate;

(b)provide any test hardware, equipment, environments and site access set out in the Proposal, and keep site visits safe, including inductions, permits and supervision required at your premises;

(c)name a contact with authority to make decisions, and make decisions and give approvals promptly; and

(d)ensure you have the rights to provide the Client Materials and for us to use them for the services.

5.2You remain responsible for the safe operation of your plant, machines, vehicles and systems, including isolation and lock-out before we connect to equipment, and for the backup of your own data and systems.

5.3If you or your contractors delay us or do not meet these responsibilities, the affected dates move by a reasonable period. We will tell you promptly. Where the delay causes us extra cost or idle time, such as rebooked travel or reserved engineering time that cannot be reassigned, we may charge it at the rates in the Proposal after telling you in writing.

6. Fees and Payment

6.1Schedule. Unless the Proposal states otherwise, fixed fees are invoiced 40% on acceptance of the Proposal, 40% on completion of the Milestone named in the Proposal, and 20% on final Acceptance. An example is set out in Schedule B. Work begins once the first invoice is paid.

6.2Due date and method. Invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date. Fees are in the currency stated in the Proposal, or US dollars if none is stated. Payment is by bank transfer or Wise to the account shown on the invoice. We will never ask you to change payment details by email alone; confirm any change with us by phone or in the Portal before paying.

6.3Bank charges. You bear your own bank and transfer charges, so that we receive the full invoiced amount. Intermediary charges are shared where your bank’s system allows that option.

6.4Late payment. If an undisputed invoice is more than 7 days overdue, we may pause work after giving you written notice, and dates move by the length of the pause. We may also charge interest on overdue amounts at 1% per month (or, if lower, the maximum rate permitted by law), from the due date until payment. If you dispute an invoice in good faith, tell us within the payment period, pay the undisputed part, and we will resolve the rest under clause 21.

6.5Taxes. Fees exclude VAT, sales tax, goods and services tax and similar taxes, which are added where applicable. If you are required by law to withhold or deduct tax from a payment, you will pay such additional amount that we receive the full sum we would have received without the withholding, unless the Proposal states otherwise. You will give us the official receipt or certificate for any amount withheld.

7. Hardware and Third-Party Components

7.1Pricing. Bill-of-materials, component, fabrication and assembly costs are passed through either at the price stated in the Proposal or at our cost plus the margin stated in the Proposal. Where component prices or exchange rates move materially before we order, we will tell you and agree the change before ordering.

Risk of loss or damage passes to you on delivery to the address in the Proposal, or on handover at your site where we install it. Until title passes, you will keep the Hardware identifiable and insured.

Delivery dates for Hardware are estimates; we will keep you informed, propose alternatives where a part is unavailable, and agree substitutes with you before using them where they could affect form, fit or function.

7.5Certification. Regulatory testing and certification (such as CE, FCC, UKCA, IC or RCM marking, safety, EMC or radio approvals) is included only if the Proposal lists it. Unless so listed, Hardware is supplied as engineering prototypes or pilot units for evaluation and controlled use, and you are responsible for any certification needed before wider sale or deployment.

7.6Import. Unless the Proposal states otherwise, you act as importer of record and are responsible for import duties, taxes and clearance in your country.

8. Acceptance

8.1When a Deliverable or Milestone is ready, we will notify you in the Portal and test it, or help you test it, against the acceptance criteria in the Proposal. Where the Proposal has no specific criteria, the test is whether the Deliverable materially conforms to its description in the Proposal.

8.2Within 5 Business Days of notification you will either accept or give us a written list of material defects, meaning failures to meet the acceptance criteria, with enough detail for us to reproduce them.

8.3The Deliverable or Milestone is treated as Accepted if (a) you do not respond within that period, or (b) you use it in production or for commercial purposes, other than for testing under this clause.

8.4We will fix listed material defects within a reasonable time and re-submit for testing under this clause, limited to the defects listed. Minor issues that do not prevent use against the acceptance criteria do not hold up Acceptance; we will fix them under clause 9.

9. Warranty

9.1For 30 days after Acceptance of a Deliverable, we will fix at no charge any reproducible defect that causes it not to conform to the specification agreed in the Proposal, provided you report it in writing within that period.

9.2The warranty does not cover defects caused by: misuse, accident or use contrary to our documentation; changes made by anyone other than us; operation in environments, hardware, operating systems or versions not listed as supported in the Proposal; Client Materials; or third-party products and services, which are covered by clause 7.4.

9.3Fixing defects under clause 9.1 is our sole obligation under this warranty. Support after the warranty period is available under a retainer or a new Proposal.

9.4Except as expressly set out in this Agreement or a Proposal, and to the fullest extent the law allows, all other warranties, conditions and terms, whether express or implied by statute, common law or otherwise, including as to satisfactory quality, merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose, are excluded. We do not warrant that software or Hardware will be error-free or operate without interruption.

11.2Each party will use the other’s Confidential Information only for the purposes of this Agreement, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to its personnel, subcontractors and professional advisers who need to know it and are bound by equivalent obligations.

11.3These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public without breach, was already known to the recipient, is independently developed, or is lawfully received from a third party. A party may disclose information where required by law or a regulator, giving the other party notice where lawful.

11.4These obligations last for 3 years after termination or expiry of the last Proposal, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

16. Term and Termination

16.1This Agreement applies from acceptance of the first Proposal until the last Proposal has ended. Each Proposal runs until its services are complete, unless terminated earlier under this clause.

16.2Convenience. Either party may terminate a Proposal on 14 days’ written notice. You will pay for work done up to the termination date, at the rates in the Proposal or pro rata to the Milestone, and for non-cancellable commitments made for you, such as components ordered or fabrication runs started. Any amount you have paid in advance for work not done is refunded.

16.3Breach. Either party may terminate a Proposal immediately by written notice if the other commits a material breach and does not cure it within 14 days of written notice describing it.

16.4Insolvency. Either party may terminate immediately by written notice if the other becomes insolvent, enters administration, liquidation or any arrangement with its creditors, or ceases to carry on business, or anything equivalent happens in any jurisdiction.

16.5Effects. On termination: (a) we will hand over all work in progress, Deliverables and Hardware you have paid for, and clause 10 applies to them; (b) each party will return or destroy the other’s Confidential Information, except copies it must keep by law or that sit in routine backups, which remain protected under clause 11; (c) we will revoke our access to your systems; and (d) accrued rights and remedies are not affected.

17. Non-Solicitation

17.1During a Proposal and for 12 months after it ends, you will not directly or indirectly employ or engage any of our employees or subcontractors who worked on that Proposal, or solicit them to leave us, without our written consent.

17.2This does not stop you hiring someone who responds to a general advertisement not targeted at our people.

17.3If you do hire or engage such a person, then as an alternative to any other remedy you will pay us a fee equal to 25% of that person’s first-year salary or annualised fees with you. The parties agree this is a reasonable estimate of our recruitment and replacement costs.

18. Independent Contractor and Subcontractors

18.1We act as an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates employment, partnership, agency or a joint venture, and neither party may bind the other.

18.2We may use vetted subcontractors, including fabrication houses, assembly partners and specialist engineers. Each is bound by confidentiality and intellectual-property terms at least as protective as this Agreement. We remain responsible for their work as if it were our own.

19. Force Majeure

19.1Neither party is liable for delay or failure to perform caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, war, terrorism, civil unrest, government action, sanctions, power or internet outages affecting a region, failures of carriers or customs, and component shortages affecting the market generally. This clause does not excuse payment of amounts due.

19.2The affected party will notify the other promptly, take reasonable steps to limit the effect, and resume performance as soon as possible. If the event continues for more than 60 days, either party may terminate the affected Proposal by written notice, and clause 16.2 applies to payment for work done.

20. Notices

20.1Notices under this Agreement are given by email to the addresses stated in the Proposal or recorded in the Portal, or by a message in the Portal. Our address for notices is er@epsilon-labs.co.

20.2A notice is received when sent, if sent before 17:00 on a Business Day in the recipient’s time zone, and otherwise at 09:00 on the next Business Day, unless the sender receives an automated message that it was not delivered. Notices of breach or termination must also be sent by email, not only in the Portal.

20.3Each party will keep its notice address up to date in the Portal.

21. Governing Law and Disputes

21.1Escalation. If a dispute arises, either party may give written notice of it. Senior representatives of both parties will then meet (in person or by video) and try in good faith to resolve it within 30 days of the notice.

21.2Arbitration. Any dispute not resolved under clause 21.1 shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre in accordance with the Arbitration Rules of the Singapore International Arbitration Centre for the time being in force. The seat of the arbitration shall be Singapore. The tribunal shall consist of one arbitrator. The language of the arbitration shall be English.

21.3Governing law. This Agreement, each Proposal and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or in connection with them are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

21.4Nothing in this clause prevents either party from seeking urgent injunctive or interim relief from any competent court, or us from bringing proceedings for an undisputed debt in the courts of the place where you are established.

22. General

22.1Entire agreement. This Agreement and each accepted Proposal form the entire agreement between the parties about their subject matter and replace all earlier proposals, discussions and understandings about it. Each party confirms it has not relied on any statement not set out in them, but nothing limits liability for fraud.

22.2Amendment. A change to this Agreement or a Proposal is effective only if made in writing and agreed by both parties. Acceptance of a revised Proposal or an approved Change Request in the Portal counts as written agreement.

22.3Assignment. Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations without the other’s written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign to a successor in a merger or sale of all or substantially all of its relevant business on written notice.

22.4Severability. If any provision is found invalid or unenforceable, it is modified to the minimum extent needed to make it enforceable, and the rest of the Agreement is unaffected.

22.5No waiver. A failure or delay in exercising a right is not a waiver of it, and a single or partial exercise does not prevent its further exercise.

22.6Third parties. No one other than the parties has any right to enforce any term of this Agreement, including under the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999.

22.7Survival. Clauses 6 (for amounts due), 7.2, 9.4, 10, 11, 12, 15, 16.5, 17, 21 and 22, and any other provision that by its nature is intended to survive, continue after termination or expiry.
