# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (template)

Template for review by qualified counsel before first use. Used before a prospect sends firmware for a CRA gap read.

**Parties.** Epsilon Labs AI Solutions (SMC-PVT) Ltd. (CUIN 0290150), trading as Epsilon Labs, 21-A Zahoor Afridi Road, Cantt, Lahore 54000, Pakistan ("Epsilon Labs"); and [Company name, registered number, address] ("Company").

**1. Purpose.** The parties are discussing whether Epsilon Labs will provide firmware security and Cyber Resilience Act engineering services, including an initial review of firmware the Company chooses to supply (the "Purpose").

**2. Confidential Information.** All non-public information one party discloses to the other for the Purpose, in any form. This includes firmware images, source code, schematics, product plans and any findings Epsilon Labs produces from the Company's materials. All such findings are the Company's Confidential Information.

**3. Obligations.** The recipient will:
- use Confidential Information only for the Purpose;
- protect it with at least reasonable care, including encrypted storage for firmware;
- share it only with its employees and contractors who need it for the Purpose and are bound by equivalent duties;
- not reverse-engineer the discloser's materials beyond what the Purpose requires.

**4. Exceptions.** The obligations do not apply to information that:
- is or becomes public without breach;
- was already known to the recipient;
- is independently developed; or
- is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

Disclosure required by law or a regulator is allowed, with prior notice where lawful.

**5. Return and deletion.** On request, or within 30 days after discussions end, the recipient deletes or returns Confidential Information. It may keep one archival copy only where law requires, under continuing confidentiality.

**6. No obligation, no licence.** Nothing here obliges either party to enter a contract or grants any licence or intellectual property right. Information is provided "as is".

**7. Term.** This agreement covers disclosures for 12 months from signature. The obligations last 3 years after the last disclosure, and for trade secrets for as long as they remain trade secrets.

**8. Law and disputes.** Governed by the laws of England and Wales. Disputes go to the courts of England and Wales, and either party may seek urgent injunctive relief in any competent court.
