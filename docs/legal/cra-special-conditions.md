# CRA Fix Sprint — Special Conditions (template)

Template for review by qualified counsel before first use. Paste into the "special conditions" of any Proposal for a CRA Fix Sprint or CRA retrofit. Under clause 1.3 of the Services Agreement (T-2026-1), these take priority over the Agreement.

**SC1. Manufacturer responsibility.** The Client is and remains the "manufacturer" of its products for the purposes of Regulation (EU) 2024/2847 (Cyber Resilience Act) and any other product law. Epsilon Labs provides engineering services and advice only. It does not act as the Client's manufacturer, authorised representative, importer, distributor or notified body, and it does not issue or sign any EU declaration of conformity.

**SC2. No compliance warranty.** Deliverables are intended to help the Client meet its obligations. Epsilon Labs does not warrant that any product, document or Deliverable makes the Client compliant with the CRA or any other law. Conformity assessment and regulatory decisions are the Client's.

**SC3. Release control.** The Client decides whether any firmware change, update or patch delivered by Epsilon Labs is released to products in the field. The Client is responsible for testing it on its production configurations before release, and for every release it makes. Epsilon Labs does not push updates to the Client's fielded devices unless a Proposal says so expressly and in writing.

**SC4. Vulnerability reports.** Any vulnerability reports and notifications under the CRA (including early warnings to the single reporting platform) are made by the Client. Where a runbook is a Deliverable, it describes the Client's process; Epsilon Labs does not file reports on the Client's behalf.

**SC5. Authorised analysis only.** The Client confirms that it owns, or has the right to have analysed, all firmware, hardware and documentation it supplies, including third-party modules it lawfully uses. Epsilon Labs analyses only Client Materials supplied for the Proposal. It does not test live systems or devices it does not hold, and does not test third-party infrastructure.

**SC6. Findings.** Vulnerability findings are the Client's Confidential Information (clause 11). Epsilon Labs will not disclose them to anyone except as clause 11 allows. Where a finding concerns a third-party component, Epsilon Labs may, with the Client's written agreement, help the Client notify that component's supplier.

**SC7. Liability.** Clause 15 applies. For clarity, losses arising from a product recall, withdrawal, regulatory fine or penalty imposed on the Client are excluded indirect or consequential losses under clause 15.2, except to the extent caused by Epsilon Labs' fraud or wilful misconduct.

**SC8. Scope of the one scoped fix.** The Proposal names the single fix included in the Sprint price. Anything found beyond it is listed in the remediation plan and quoted separately (Change Request or new Proposal).

**SC9. Firmware handling.** Client firmware and hardware are kept on encrypted storage, used only for the Proposal, and deleted or returned within 30 days of final Acceptance unless the Client asks in writing for longer retention.
