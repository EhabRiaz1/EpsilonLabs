# Referral ask

Founder sends these personally. Agent may draft; founder edits and sends.
Never mass-send. One person, one note.

**Rules that do not bend**
- The fee is 10% of the first-project invoice, paid when the client pays.
- Paid to firms and independent contacts only. Never to an employee of the
  client or prospect company. If the person works there, do not mention a fee.
  Send the "no fee" line instead (bottom of file).
- No prices in the note. Quotes come after a call.
- No deadlines, no "limited spots".

---

## Variant A: past client

Subject: What Epsilon Labs does now

Hi {{first_name}},

Since we worked on {{project}}, Epsilon Labs has narrowed to three things:

1. Fuel retail: eFuelPro and forecourt automation.
2. Industrial monitoring and retrofits: mills, factories, generators, cold chain.
3. Decoding machine protocols that have no documentation, remotely if needed.

I am not asking you to buy anything. If you know someone with one of these
problems, an introduction is all I need. I will take it from there.

If the introduction comes from your own firm or as an independent contact, and
they become a client, I pay 10% of their first-project invoice once they have
paid it. If that does not suit you, no hard feelings.

Thanks,
Ehab

*(~125 words)*
