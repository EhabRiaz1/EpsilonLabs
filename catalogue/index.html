Catalogue

What you can hire us for.

Every one of these has a written scope, a typical length, and a line saying who it is not for. Prices are not published: we quote the work after a call, because a number without a scope is a guess.

01 Engagements

Fixed scope, fixed fee, a written deliverable and a clean ending.

  1. Pre-Fab Design Review Find the board-killer before you pay for a hundred of them. 3–5 daysContact sales
  2. Protocol Decode Sprint Your machine is already talking. We work out what it is saying. 2–4 weeksContact sales
  3. Board-to-Fab Schematic to fabrication-ready, owned by you. 6–10 weeksContact sales
  4. Firmware Rescue It half-works, and whoever wrote it is gone. 3–6 weeksContact sales

02 Security

Hardware and authorisation, not compliance sign-off. We test devices and data isolation; we are not an accredited firm and will say so.

  1. Device Penetration Test We take your device apart the way an attacker would. 2–3 weeksContact sales
  2. Authorisation Model Review Can tenant A read tenant B's row? We find out properly. 1 weekContact sales

  3. Datasheet to Footprint A footprint drawn from the mechanical drawing, not from the internet. 2–3 daysContact sales
