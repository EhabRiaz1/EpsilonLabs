Software · Booking & Scheduling

Stop selling slots. Start filling them.

Online booking, point of sale and a scheduler that packs time to maximise occupancy — for courts, rooms, chairs, bays or anything else people reserve by the hour. Your brand and your customers, not a marketplace's.

Booking & Scheduling

Typical length
4–6 weeks
What you get
6 written deliverables, listed below
What decides the number
Number of sites and bookable resources, whether you need memberships and packages, and which payment provider you settle through.
Scoping

Contact salesGet a quote

01 The terms

Quoted per project, after a call. We do not publish a band for this, because a number without a scope is a guess and you would have to unpick it later anyway.

Tell us what the work has to do and when it has to be done, and you get the scope and the number in writing — or a straight answer that this is not ours.

02 Try it

Interactive demo

Click to book a slot, then let the scheduler fill the dead hours between bookings.

03 The problem

An empty hour between two bookings is revenue that never comes back, and it is almost never sold on its own. Courts, treatment rooms, studios, salon chairs, service bays — the economics are identical.

Marketplace apps fill the gaps by owning your customer. You pay a percentage forever and your regulars book through somebody else's brand.

04 What you get

  • Online booking on your own domain, in your brand
  • A scheduler that packs bookings to close the dead gaps between them
  • Point of sale for the front desk, with real-time stock deduction
  • Memberships, packages, recurring bookings and waitlists
  • Occupancy and revenue dashboard, per resource and per hour
  • Your customer list, exportable, owned by you

05 How it runs

Week 1
Your resources, pricing, peak hours and packages mapped.
Weeks 2–4
Booking, scheduler and POS built against your real configuration.
Weeks 5–6
Staff trained, live alongside your old system, then cut over.

06 Whether this is for you

This is for you if

  • Sports facilities, clinics, studios, salons, workshops, co-working spaces
  • Anyone paying a marketplace a percentage of every booking
  • Venues with a shop or cafe that needs one till, not two

This is not for you if

  • Anyone who wants a marketplace's customer discovery — we build your system, not a network
  • A single resource, where a booking calendar is enough
  • Airline, hotel or ticketing-scale inventory

07 Proof

The Courts runs booking and point of sale for a padel and pickleball facility: a scheduler that packs court time to maximise utilisation, real-time stock deduction, and sub-100 ms availability queries across historical data.
