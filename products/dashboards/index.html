Software · Sales & Admin Dashboards

Every number you check, in one place, live.

The dashboard your business should already have: revenue, orders, margins and stock, pulled live from the systems you already run, with the admin tools to act on what you see.

Sales & Admin Dashboards

Typical length
3–4 weeks
What you get
6 written deliverables, listed below
What decides the number
How many systems the numbers come from and how clean they are. One database is at the floor; a POS, an accounting package and three spreadsheets that disagree is where the time goes.
Scoping

01 The terms

Quoted per project, after a call. We do not publish a band for this, because a number without a scope is a guess and you would have to unpick it later anyway.

Tell us what the work has to do and when it has to be done, and you get the scope and the number in writing — or a straight answer that this is not ours.

02 Try it

Interactive demo

Switch period and region; revenue, orders and the chart update live.

03 The problem

The numbers exist. They are in the POS, the accounting package, the e-commerce admin and a spreadsheet somebody updates on Fridays. Answering "how did we do last week" takes an afternoon, so it gets asked once a month.

And the dashboards that ship with each tool only show that tool's half of the picture.

04 What you get

  • Live revenue, orders, average order value and margin, by period and by region
  • Every source connected once — POS, accounting, store, database
  • Drill-down from any figure to the transactions behind it
  • Admin tools to act on what you see: refunds, stock, customers
  • Role-based access, so each manager sees their own numbers
  • Scheduled email and WhatsApp summaries

05 How it runs

Week 1
Every source mapped, and the numbers reconciled against your books.
Weeks 2–3
Dashboard and admin tools built against live data.
Week 4
Roles, alerts, handover.

06 Whether this is for you

This is for you if

  • Retail, hospitality, distribution and e-commerce businesses
  • Owners who find out how last month went in the middle of this one
  • Teams whose reporting lives in exported spreadsheets

This is not for you if

  • Enterprise data warehouses and BI programmes
  • Businesses whose only system already has good reporting
  • Reporting on data you cannot get read access to

07 Proof

eFuelPro's dashboard shows per-nozzle, per-shift and per-day sales read straight off the dispenser, viewable from anywhere. It is our own product rather than a client dashboard over a POS and accounting package — ask us for the specifics of a build like yours.
