Cookies

We do not use cookies.

Or anything like them. That is why there is no banner asking you to accept any.

Last updated 14 October 2026

What that means in practice

This site sets no cookies, and stores nothing else on your device either — no local storage, session storage or similar. It loads no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or social-media scripts, and it requests no files from other companies’ servers: the fonts, scripts and images all come from this domain.

We count visits with a small script of our own. It stores nothing on your device: each visit is told apart by an anonymous code the server works out from your network address and browser with a key that changes daily, and neither is kept. It records nothing at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The privacy policy lists exactly what it records.

The opening animation on the home page plays when you arrive from elsewhere and skips when you return to it from another page here. It decides that from the page you came from, which your browser sends with every request, not from anything saved on your device.

Links to other sites

A few pages link to client and partner websites. Those sites have their own cookie and privacy policies, which apply once you follow the link.

If this changes

If we ever add something that stores information on your device and is not strictly necessary for the site to work, we will ask for your consent before it runs, and this page will list it first.

How we handle the emails you send us is in the privacy policy.
