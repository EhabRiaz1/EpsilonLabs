Tell us what you are building.

A few questions, one at a time. At the end you get the offer it maps to and an email ready to send.

Email er@epsilon-labs.co and include:

  1. Software, hardware, or both
  2. What the work is, and what exists today
  3. When it has to be done, and the budget band
  4. The constraint you are most worried about

If there is hardware involved, a model number and a photograph of the connector will save us both a week.
